### Surgery Overview
Surgery is a medical specialty that involves the use of manual or instrumental techniques to repair, remove, or manipulate damaged or diseased tissues and organs. It is a broad field that encompasses various subspecialties, including:

* **General Surgery**: deals with a wide range of surgical procedures, such as appendectomies, hernia repairs, and gallbladder removals.
* **Cardiothoracic Surgery**: focuses on surgical procedures related to the heart, lungs, and chest cavity.
* **Neurosurgery**: involves surgical procedures related to the brain, spine, and nervous system.
* **Plastic Surgery**: deals with reconstructive and cosmetic surgical procedures, such as skin grafts, facelifts, and breast augmentations.

### Types of Surgery
There are several types of surgery, including:

* **Elective Surgery**: scheduled surgical procedures that are not emergency-related, such as cosmetic surgery or joint replacement.
* **Emergency Surgery**: urgent surgical procedures that require immediate attention, such as appendectomies or trauma-related surgeries.
* **Minimally Invasive Surgery**: surgical procedures that use small incisions and specialized instruments to minimize tissue damage and promote faster recovery.
* **Robotic Surgery**: uses robotic systems to assist surgeons during surgical procedures, enhancing precision and dexterity.

### Surgical Procedures
Some common surgical procedures include:

* **Appendectomy**: removal of the appendix.
* **Cholecystectomy**: removal of the gallbladder.
* **Herniorrhaphy**: repair of a hernia.
* **Mastectomy**: removal of one or both breasts.
* **Craniotomy**: surgical procedure that involves temporarily removing a portion of the skull to access the brain.

### Pre-Surgical Preparation
Before undergoing surgery, patients typically undergo:

* **Medical Evaluation**: a thorough medical examination to assess overall health and identify potential risks.
* **Imaging Tests**: diagnostic tests, such as X-rays, CT scans, or MRI scans, to visualize the affected area.
* **Laboratory Tests**: blood tests and other laboratory tests to assess blood chemistry, blood count, and other vital signs.
* **Medication Management**: review and adjustment of medications to minimize potential interactions or complications.

### Post-Surgical Care
After surgery, patients typically require:

* **Pain Management**: medication and other therapies to manage pain and discomfort.
* **Wound Care**: dressing changes, wound cleaning, and monitoring for signs of infection.
* **Physical Therapy**: exercises and rehabilitation to promote healing, strength, and mobility.
* **Follow-up Care**: scheduled appointments with the surgeon to monitor progress and address concerns.

For more information on surgery, please visit:
* [American College of Surgeons](https://www.facs.org/)
* [National Institute of Diabetes and Digestive and Kidney Diseases](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/)
